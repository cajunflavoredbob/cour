01 · Principles

the chrome is the gallery wall

cour is a quarterly ritual: a small group works through the season's anime lineup and locks in verdicts. The UI's job is to hold extremely colorful third-party art without competing with it — quiet, paper-dark, typographic. The product's heartbeat is the turning of seasons, expressed through one rotating accent.

art owns colorBackgrounds stay near-neutral. Saturated color appears only in the seasonal accent, the four-season mark, and the posters themselves.
one accent at a timeA single seasonal hue is live per cour. It marks positive/brand moments: Like, focus, progress, links, the season chip.
negative ≠ brandDestructive/negative actions use clay — never the accent, never reely's retired pink.
mono is the voice of dataEvery meta line, label, count, and timestamp is Geist Mono, uppercase, letterspaced. Serif is for moments; mono is for facts.
no ratings pre-airThe deck shows upcoming titles. Popularity/score numbers never appear in user-facing UI.
buttons, not gestures, decideVerdicts are explicit button presses. Gestures (drawer swipe) only reveal information, never commit it.

neverThe reely pink→amber gradient is retired. No gradients as brand color anywhere; the only gradients are photographic scrims.

04 · Mark & lockup

two arcs in conversation

The large ivory arc is the c; the answer comes back as the four seasons. Static — it never updates. Full spec incl. canonical SVG lives in the handoff (APP_MARK.md).

cour SUMMER 2026

Stylized wordmark — the mark IS the c

our our
constructionThe arc-c replaces the letter c: mark rendered at ~0.9× the cap height of “our” (Shippori Mincho 600), optically centered on the x-height, 2px gap before the o. The dots read as the c's counter.
small sizesBelow ~34px word height, use the fused small-variant arc (gray answering arc, no dots) — same rule as the standalone mark.
when to useSplash, auth masthead, marketing. In dense chrome (topbars), prefer plain-type “cour” + season chip — the stylized c gets noisy beside other icons.
< 48px renderedSwap to the small variant: dots fuse into a single gray answering arc. Never just scale the dots.
dot colorsFixed — never re-tinted to the current season.
season chipThe 夏/秋/冬/春 chip is a separate element (serif, accent-hi text, 1px line-strong border, 5–6px radius). It rotates; the mark doesn't.

06 · Spacing & shape

round pills, soft cards, safe bottoms

radii999px buttons/pills/chips · 16px cards, inputs, media · 22–28px sheets · 8px small thumbs · icon tile ≈ 23% of size.
gutters20–24px screen edges · 28px auth screens.
hit targets44px minimum; verdict buttons 52px. Text-only Skip keeps the full 116×52 invisible target.
safe area34px bottom padding under the last interactive row (iOS home indicator). The lip's swipe zone must clear the OS gesture zone.
elevationBorders and fills, not shadows. The only shadows: poster cards and the accent glow under primary/Like buttons (accent @ 45–55%, y 8, blur 24–32, spread −8/−10).
07 · Motion & sound

small moves, honest defaults

card advanceVerdict press → current poster exits (fade + slight scale-down), next fades in. No directional fling — direction implied swiping, which is gone.
drawerSpring, ~320ms, from the lip's resting position. Poster behind dims to brightness 0.5 / saturate 0.8.
skip hold1.5s linear fill, label counts down, completion skips all remaining. Release = cancel + single skip.
media rotation3s per item, crossfade ~240ms, pauses while PV plays or an item is pinned.
soundPVs always start muted ("TAP FOR SOUND" chip). One global sound toggle (topbar + account sheet) owns theme music and PV default; persisted per user. Prefer one ambient seasonal loop over per-card themes.
reduced motionRespect prefers-reduced-motion: replace springs/fills with fades; keep the skip-hold countdown as a visible timer.
08 · Voice

lowercase moments, uppercase facts

Do

Serif headlines in lowercase: "the season is waiting."
Mono system messages in caps: SCORES ARE TALLIED ONCE EVERYONE LOCKS IN
Verbs on buttons: sign in, lock in, keep picking.

Don't

No match/dating language: no "it's a match!", no flames, no swipe verbs.
No exclamation-mark enthusiasm; the season chip does the celebrating.
No numbers theater — verdict math (+1/0/−1) stays invisible in UI.
